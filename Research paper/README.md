# Research Paper

## Literature Review – AI-Based Job Recommendation and Career Guidance Platform

This folder contains research papers and references relevant to the project's literature review.

### Research Focus

The project research focuses on:

- Efficient job searching
- Identifying jobs related to user skills
- Prioritizing suitable job opportunities
- Organizing job records efficiently
- Representing relationships between users, skills, and jobs

### Selected Research Papers

#### 1. Skills2Job: A Recommender System that Encodes Job Offer Embeddings on Graph Databases

**Authors:** Relevant research team in labor-market intelligence and recommendation systems  
**Published:** 2021, *Applied Soft Computing*  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.asoc.2020.107049

**Why it is relevant:** This paper is especially relevant to the project because it uses a graph database to represent labor-market information and recommends suitable occupations from users' skills. It directly supports the project's Graph and skill-job relationship concept.

**Paper:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asoc.2020.107049

---

#### 2. A Novel Approach for Job Matching and Skill Recommendation Using Transformers and the O*NET Database

**Published:** 2025, *Big Data Research*  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.bdr.2025.100509

**Why it is relevant:** The paper studies matching candidate resumes with jobs using skills and job descriptions and also recommends skills/courses needed to become eligible for particular jobs. This connects strongly with the project's job matching and career-guidance objectives.

**Paper:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bdr.2025.100509

---

#### 3. Topic Modeling Driven Content Based Jobs Recommendation Engine for Recruitment Industry

**Published:** 2017, *Procedia Computer Science*  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.procs.2017.11.448

**Why it is relevant:** This research addresses the same core problem of connecting users with relevant job postings by matching skills and preferences with job features. It provides useful background for the project's job-searching and matching component.

**Paper:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.procs.2017.11.448

---

#### 4. SkillRec: A Data-Driven Approach to Job Skill Recommendation for Career Insights

**Authors:** Xiang Qian Ong and Kwan Hui Lim  
**Published:** 2023  
**arXiv:** 2302.09938

**Why it is relevant:** SkillRec recommends skills required for a job from job titles and job descriptions. It is useful for the career-guidance side of the project and for understanding how job descriptions can be used to identify relevant skills.

**Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09938

---
