# Research Paper

## Literature Review – AI-Based Job Recommendation and Career Guidance Platform

This folder contains research sources relevant to the project's job recommendation, skill matching, graph-based representation, and career guidance focus.

## Selected Research Papers

### 1. Skills2Job: A Recommender System that Encodes Job Offer Embeddings on Graph Databases

**Authors:** Anna Giabelli, Lorenzo Malandri, Fabio Mercorio, Mario Mezzanzanica, Andrea Seveso  
**Year:** 2021  
**Journal:** *Applied Soft Computing*, Volume 101, Article 107049  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.asoc.2020.107049

**Why it is relevant:** The paper uses graph-based representation of labor-market information and recommends suitable occupations from users' skills. This connects directly with the project's planned Graph/Adjacency List representation of relationships between users, skills, and jobs.

**Paper / PDF:** https://feast-ecmlpkdd.github.io/archive/2021/papers/FEAST2021_paper_2.pdf  
**Publisher:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S156849462030987X

---

### 2. A Novel Approach for Job Matching and Skill Recommendation Using Transformers and the O*NET Database

**Authors:** Rubén Alonso, Danilo Dessì, Antonello Meloni, Diego Reforgiato Recupero  
**Year:** 2025  
**Journal:** *Big Data Research*, Volume 39, Article 100509  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.bdr.2025.100509

**Why it is relevant:** The research addresses job matching and skill recommendation, making it directly relevant to the project's objective of identifying suitable jobs from user skills and supporting career guidance.

**Paper / institutional source:** https://iris.unica.it/handle/11584/480226  
**Publisher:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214579625000048

---

### 3. Topic Modeling Driven Content Based Jobs Recommendation Engine for Recruitment Industry

**Authors:** S. B. V. G. N. S. Kumar, K. S. K. R. Rao, and co-authors  
**Year:** 2017  
**Journal:** *Procedia Computer Science*  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.procs.2017.11.448

**Why it is relevant:** This research addresses content-based job recommendation by connecting user skills and preferences with job-posting information. It provides background for the project's job-searching, matching, and recommendation components.

**Paper:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.procs.2017.11.448  
**Publisher:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877050917326960

---

### 4. SkillRec: A Data-Driven Approach to Job Skill Recommendation for Career Insights

**Authors:** Xiang Qian Ong and Kwan Hui Lim  
**Year:** 2023  
**arXiv:** 2302.09938

**Why it is relevant:** SkillRec studies how job titles and descriptions can be used to recommend relevant skills. This supports the career-guidance side of the project and the relationship between jobs and required skills.

**Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09938  
**PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.09938

## DSA Areas Supported by the Literature Review

The selected papers provide research context for the project's focus on:

- Graph and relationship representation
- Skill-job matching
- Efficient job searching and organization
- Recommendation and prioritization
- Career guidance through relevant skills

> These papers are literature sources supporting the project topic. They do not imply that the project itself uses the same AI/ML methods; the project's implementation remains based on the DSA concepts defined in the PBL report.
